How the axes were measured

Hours. Every Claude Code transcript for this repository (main sessions and their subagents) was merged into one timeline; the horizontal axis is the running sum of gaps between consecutive events, counting a gap only when it is under 30 minutes. This counts wall-clock time during which an agent was active, not tokens or human attention. The time climb spans 0–12.6 h (Sep 25–26); the reset on Oct 19 adds 0.4 h; climb 2 spans 13.0–65.9 h (Oct 20–22).

Solving time. Each point is one accepted reference CSV at its file time, or for climb 1 the ledger's figure at the time the transcript shows the step accepted. Climb 1 values are "valid time" on the 454 provable problems, which includes failures at the cap (the first point has 11 failures). Climb 2 values are summed from the CSVs of the seven-division run and sit 1.5–2.5 s below the standalone measurements in the ledger.

Problems proved. Of the 454 provable problems in two divisions until 13.7 h, then of the 788 valid problems in seven divisions.

Lines of code. wc -l of Sep/Xsearch.lean, Sep/SegC.lean and the xsimpl driver (Sep/Xsimpl/Core.lean, or Sep/SepLogic.lean before the split on Oct 20) at each step's commit. Climb 1 commits were removed when the repository was reset on Oct 19, so the line series starts at the T6 end state; the ledger records only Xsearch.lean at some earlier steps (5885 lines at step 19, 6381 at step 22).

Not on the chart. The proof climb that preceded the time climb (steps 1–20, 2026-09-22 to 09-24, 289 → 454 problems proved on a Mac) has no transcripts on this machine and no comparable times. The raw rows and these notes are kept in ledgers/time-climb-data.md beside this page.
